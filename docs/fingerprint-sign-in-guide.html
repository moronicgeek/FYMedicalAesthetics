Sign in with your fingerprint

Touch the fingerprint sensor instead of typing your password. You set it up once on each phone or tablet you use, and it takes about two minutes. Your password keeps working as a back-up.

Before you start

The device has a fingerprint or screen lock

The phone or tablet must already unlock with a fingerprint, face or PIN, set up in its own Settings. The portal uses the device's lock to check it's you.

You are on the portal in Chrome

Open portal.lumiereclinic.co.za in Chrome. If you use a different browser app and you can't see the fingerprint button, tell the practice administrator.

You know your password

You type it once during set-up to prove it's you. If you have forgotten it, ask the practice administrator to reset it first.

You have the device in your own hands

Do this yourself, with your own finger, when no client is holding the tablet.

Once on each device

Set it up in five steps

  1. Sign in as usual

    Open the portal and sign in with your email address and password, the way you always do.

    The Sign in with fingerprint button is already on this screen, but it only works after set-up.

  2. Tap My account

    In the dark menu bar, tap My account. It is the last item in the menu.

  3. Name the device and type your password

    Under Fingerprint sign-in, give this device a name you will recognise, such as Reception tablet or My phone. Then type your password.

    The name helps you and the administrator know which device it is later, for example if a phone is lost.

  4. Tap the button and touch the sensor

    Tap Set up fingerprint sign-in. The phone or tablet opens its own window asking you to confirm it's you. Touch the fingerprint sensor. Some devices ask for your face or the device PIN instead.

    The device may say Create a passkey. A passkey is the name for this kind of sign-in, so tap Continue. If the browser offers to save your password, tap Never.

  5. Look for the green message

    You will see Done. Next time, tap "Sign in with fingerprint" on this device. The device now appears in the list underneath.

    That's it. Repeat these steps on any other phone or tablet you use. There is no limit.

Every day after that

Signing in with your fingerprint

On the sign-in screen

  • Tap Sign in with fingerprint. You don't type your email.
  • Touch the sensor when the device asks.
  • You go straight to Today.
  • If more than one person has set it up on the tablet, the device asks you to choose an account. Always choose your own name.

This also works on the Put this tablet into check-in mode screen, so you can hand the tablet to clients without typing your password.

Taking the tablet back from a client

When the client has finished, the tablet shows Staff only. Tap Sign in with fingerprint and touch the sensor to get back into the portal.

Quick answers

If something goes wrong

You can always sign in with your email and password instead. The messages below are the exact words the portal shows.

"That password is not correct."

Type your password again, carefully. It is the same password you sign in with. If you have forgotten it, ask the practice administrator to reset it.

"This device can't do fingerprint sign-in, or no fingerprint or screen lock is set up on it."

Open the device's own Settings and add a fingerprint or a screen lock PIN. Then come back to My account and refresh the page. Some older devices can't do it at all; keep using your password on those.

"Fingerprint sign-in is already set up for you on this device."

Nothing to do. You can already sign in with your fingerprint here.

"Setting up was cancelled." or "Fingerprint sign-in was cancelled."

The device's window was closed or timed out before you touched the sensor. Tap the button again and touch the sensor when the window appears.

"Fingerprint sign-in isn't set up for this web address."

Check the address bar says portal.lumiereclinic.co.za. If it does, use your password and tell the practice administrator.

"Fingerprint sign-in didn't work in this browser."

Use your password for now and tell the practice administrator. A password manager app or add-on in the browser can block fingerprint sign-in.

I can't see the "Sign in with fingerprint" button

The button only shows on devices that can do it. Check the device has a fingerprint or screen lock, and that you are using Chrome. Otherwise sign in with your password.

I got a new phone, or my fingerprint stopped working

Sign in with your password and set it up again on the new phone. On My account, tap Remove next to the old phone's name.

I lost my phone

Tell the practice administrator straight away so they can remove it. If you can, sign in on another device, open My account and tap Remove next to the lost phone's name.

Patient records are confidential

Keep it safe

Never

  • Set up fingerprint sign-in for someone else, or let them use yours.
  • Set it up while a client is holding the tablet.
  • Let the browser save your password on the tablet. Tap Never if it asks.
  • Choose a colleague's name when the tablet asks which account to use.

Good to know

  • Your fingerprint stays on the device. The clinic never receives it.
  • Your password still works. Keep it private.
  • Every sign-in is recorded in the access log.
  • Sign out when you leave a device you share.
For the practice administrator

Looking after fingerprint sign-in

You can see and remove anyone's devices.

Removing a device

Open Staff. Under each person you will see Fingerprint sign-in on: and their device names. Tap Remove next to a device when it is lost, replaced, or the person leaves. Their password still works until you remove their access.

The shared tablet

The tablet accepts any fingerprint saved in its own Settings, and its screen-lock PIN. Anyone who can unlock the tablet can confirm a fingerprint sign-in on it, so only add staff fingerprints in the tablet's Settings, and keep its PIN among staff. Never add a client's fingerprint.
