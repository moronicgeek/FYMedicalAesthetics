Why it matters

The system is part of the treatment

The portal is not extra paperwork. Each step protects the client, the doctor and you. Skipping one step can make a treatment unsafe or leave the clinic without proof of consent.

It stops unsafe drips

The vitals are checked against safe ranges before anything goes to the doctor. If one reading is out of range, the screen says stop and refer, and the client signs nothing.

A doctor approves every drip

No IV drip starts without a doctor's YES. The doctor sees the client's first name, age, drip and vitals on WhatsApp, and the answer is saved on the case.

Signed consent, every time

Clients sign on the tablet. A PDF copy is emailed to them and to hello@lumiereclinic.co.za straight away, so the clinic always has proof of what the client agreed to.

Private by law (POPIA)

Health details are encrypted and stored safely. Every look at a patient's record is logged with who opened it. Staff see only what their role needs.

Your role

Reception

You are the first and last face the client sees. You start every visit on the tablet and keep the waiting list moving. You do not treat clients.

A visit, step by step

  1. Greet the client. On Check in, tap Hand tablet to client. The tablet locks to the client start screen.
  2. Give them the tablet. They tap their treatment: IV drip, Botox, filler, peel or microneedling, or Laser.
  3. IV drip: they type their name, ID number, contact number and emergency contact, choose a drip and they are in the queue. Injections or laser: they fill in the whole consent form and sign it.
  4. Take the tablet back. Tap Staff: exit check-in mode and sign in with your fingerprint (or password).
  5. Check that the client shows on the Waiting list on Today, and let the practitioner or doctor know.
  6. Book any follow-up on Appointments. Search for the client first. Mark people as arrived, completed or did not attend on Today.

Good to know

  • If a client struggles, you can type the details in for them on Check in.
  • The A+ buttons on the tablet make the text bigger for older clients.
  • A returning client is recognised from their ID number.
  • Every client needs an emergency contact name and number.

Never

  • Leave the tablet unlocked on a staff screen with a client.
  • Let a browser save your password on the tablet.
  • Share your sign-in, or sign in for someone else.

Your role

Practitioner

You run IV drips from the queue to the close. Injections, peels, microneedling and laser are doctor-only, so you will not see those clients in the portal.

An IV drip, step by step

  1. Pick the client from the Waiting list on Today or Treatments. The details they gave at check-in are already filled in.
  2. Check the details with them. The date of birth comes from the ID number. You can take a photo of their ID.
  3. Go through the medical history questions together, and confirm the drip.
  4. Take the vitals: blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate and temperature (oxygen is optional). Choose the doctor and tap Submit and check vitals.
  5. Wait for the doctor. The screen updates by itself when they reply. NO, or a reading out of range: stop. Do not give the drip. Refer the client and close the case with a note.
  6. On a YES, tap Hand the tablet to the patient. They read and sign the consent. Take the tablet back with your fingerprint.
  7. Give the drip. Afterwards, record the second set of vitals with the drip's batch number and expiry.
  8. Close the case with a short note (up to 200 characters) and your signature.

Good to know

  • If the doctor phones you their YES, you may record it, but you must give the doctor's name and the reason.
  • If a client leaves before being seen, remove them from the waiting list.
  • The client signs only after the doctor says YES.

Never

  • Start a drip without a doctor's YES on the case.
  • Carry on when the screen says a reading is out of range.
  • Leave a case open at the end of the day.

Your role

Doctor

You approve every IV drip, and you treat and close every injection, peel, microneedling and laser visit.

Approving an IV drip

  1. A WhatsApp message arrives with the client's first name and surname initial, age, drip, vitals and a link.
  2. Reply YES or NO with the case number, or tap the link and choose. The practitioner sees your answer straight away.

Injections, peels, microneedling and laser

  1. The client has already filled in and signed their form on the tablet. The case shows as Waiting for the doctor on Today.
  2. Open the case and read the client's answers to the medical questions before you treat.
  3. Treat the client.
  4. Close the case: tick the treatment you gave, add the product or batch, any products sold, your notes and your signature. The treatment appears on the consent PDF.

Good to know

  • The approval link works once and expires after four hours.
  • Replies are only accepted from your own WhatsApp number.
  • Each morning you get a list of your patients for the next day.

Never

  • Forward the WhatsApp approval message. It holds patient details.
  • Treat before reading the client's answers.

Your role

Administrator

You can do everything the others do. You also look after staff accounts, clinic settings and the access log.

Your regular jobs

  1. New staff: add them on Staff with the right role. Remove fingerprints there when someone leaves or loses a device.
  2. Settings: keep the safe vital ranges, the on-call doctor and the IV drip list up to date. The medical lead should agree the ranges.
  3. Access log: check it now and then for anything unusual, such as late-night sign-ins or many patient records opened.
  4. Signed consents: download any signed form as a PDF from the patient's record. Downloads are logged.
  5. Overview: see how many clients came in by day, week or month.

Good to know

  • Five wrong passwords lock an account for 15 minutes.
  • Sign-ins last up to 8 hours.
  • Each staff member sets up their own fingerprint on My account.

Never

  • Give someone a role higher than their job needs.
  • Export patient lists to personal email or USB sticks.

Quick answers

When something is different

My fingerprint does not work on the tablet

Sign in with your email and password instead. You can add the fingerprint again on My account.

The client gave the tablet back half way through

Hand it back to them. Their answers stay on the form until they finish. If they no longer want the treatment, remove them from the waiting list.

The doctor has not replied

Call the doctor. If they say YES on the phone, record it on the case with their name and the reason. Do not start the drip until it is recorded.

The client is here for a consultation, not a treatment

On the tablet start screen, they tap "Register your details". Book them on Appointments.

I opened the wrong patient's record

Close it. Every view is logged, which is normal. Tell the administrator if you think someone opened a record they should not have.
